Who will care for you

A team that walks with you

For more than 60 years, Eden Valley has helped people with cancer and chronic disease rebuild their health in body, mind, and spirit.

Our team includes a licensed medical doctor, licensed massage therapists, and certified health coaches. You'll see them every day: at treatments, at meals, in lectures, and in the quiet conversations in between.

We work alongside your own physician, never instead of them. The goal is for you to go home knowing what to do, and why.

Common questions

What does the $100 do?

It holds your seat in the month you choose at the webinar guest price of $5,000. The $100 comes off your program fee, so the balance is $4,900.

Who is this program for?

People facing cancer or Lyme disease who want to take an active part in their own healing. Our admissions team talks with every guest before they come, to make sure the program is a good fit for where you are.

Do I have to stop my current treatment?

No. Our program is designed to support your body alongside your medical care. Any change to your treatment belongs in a conversation with your own doctor.

How do I get there?

Fly into Denver International Airport. Plan to land by 11:30 AM on the first day of your session. We offer free pickup at 11:30 AM and bring you to our campus in Loveland, Colorado. For the trip home, book a flight between 1:00 and 3:00 PM on the last day. Our admissions team will confirm your exact dates.

Can a family member come with me?

Yes. Many guests bring a spouse or friend as a support person. Support persons stay with you, share the meals, lectures, and exercise, and have their own rate. Ask our admissions team for details.

What if my month is full?

Each month has 12 seats. If the one you want is full, choose another month, or call our admissions team at (970) 669-7730 and we'll help you find a fit.
