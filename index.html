Who it's for

Is this webinar for you?

  • You or someone you love has been diagnosed with cancer or another serious illness, whether it was last week or years ago.
  • You feel pushed to decide quickly and want to understand your options first.
  • You want to know what you can do yourself with food, rest, and daily habits, alongside your medical care.
  • You're caring for a spouse, parent, or friend and want to help them well.

Most people leave the doctor's office with one plan and very little explanation of why. They aren't told what else exists, or how their own daily habits fit in.

Barbara starts with how the body works. Then she walks through each option and the questions worth asking about it, so you can make a decision together with your own doctor.

What you'll learn

Five things Barbara will cover

1

Why the body breaks down

Disease has causes. Barbara shows you how to look for what may have contributed, and why removing a cause matters as much as treating a symptom.

2

Your options, including one few people hear about

Most people hear about the same few treatment paths. Barbara covers those, plus one that is rarely explained. For each one, she covers what it involves and what to ask about it.

3

How the body heals when it gets what it needs

Nutrition, rest, water, sunlight, fresh air, exercise, and trust. Barbara explains how each of these supports the body's own repair, in plain and practical terms.

4

Working with your doctor

How to talk with your oncologist or physician, what to bring to appointments, and where lifestyle support fits. It adds to your medical care. It doesn't replace it.

5

Your first 30 days

A short, ordered plan for the weeks after a diagnosis: what to do first, what can wait, and how to look after your mind and spirit while you decide.

Yes. Eden Valley offers these EDEN Life webinars free as a ministry to people facing a diagnosis. We ask you to register so we can send your join link and plan the Q&A.

What if I can't attend live?

Please register anyway. Coming live is best, because it's the only way to have your question answered. We'd still like you registered even if you aren't sure you can make it.

Is this only about cancer?

Cancer is the main topic. The same principles about causes, options, and supporting the body also apply to other serious conditions, such as diabetes, heart disease, and autoimmune disease.

Will Barbara tell me to stop my treatment?

No. The webinar is about understanding your options and supporting your body alongside your doctor's care. Any change to your treatment is a decision to make with your doctor.

Can I invite family or my church group?

Yes, please share this page. Each person should register with their own email so they get their own link. Many people like to watch together and talk about it afterward.
