Who it's for

Is this webinar for you?

  • You or someone you love has been diagnosed with cancer or another serious illness, whether it was last week or years ago.
  • You feel pushed to decide quickly and want to understand your options first.
  • You want to know what you can do yourself with food, rest, and daily habits, alongside your medical care.
  • You're caring for a spouse, parent, or friend and want to help them well.

A diagnosis can feel like the ground moves under you, and like every decision has to be made at once. In this free live webinar, Shanniel Fisher interviews Barbara O'Neill about the options people actually have after a diagnosis, including the one almost no one explains. She'll cover how the body was designed to respond when it is supported well, and how to think clearly about next steps, alongside your own physician.

It is the same ground Eden Valley's own EDEN Framework is built on: the four-step approach that has brought healing to many people. For cancer, and for serious disease conditions more broadly.

What you'll learn

Five things Barbara will cover

1

Why the body breaks down

Disease has causes. Barbara shows you how to look for what may have contributed, and why removing a cause matters as much as treating a symptom.

2

Your options, including one few people hear about

Most people hear about the same few treatment paths. Barbara covers those, plus one that is rarely explained. For each one, she covers what it involves and what to ask about it.

3

How the body heals when it gets what it needs

Nutrition, rest, water, sunlight, fresh air, exercise, and trust. Barbara explains how each of these supports the body's own repair, in plain and practical terms.

4

Working with your doctor

How to talk with your oncologist or physician, what to bring to appointments, and where lifestyle support fits. It adds to your medical care. It doesn't replace it.

5

Your first 30 days

A short, ordered plan for the weeks after a diagnosis: what to do first, what can wait, and how to look after your mind and spirit while you decide.

Questions

Common questions

Is the webinar really free?

Yes, completely free. Eden Valley hosts these EDEN Life webinars as a ministry to people facing a diagnosis. There is no ticket cost, and registration is simply how we send you your join link and plan for the live Q&A.

What time is it where I live?

7:00 AM Mountain is 6:00 AM Pacific, 8:00 AM Central, and 9:00 AM Eastern. Internationally, that's 2:00 PM in London and 6:00 PM in Dubai, Barbara's time, where she'll be joining from. Use the time-zone selector near the top of this page to check your own zone, and your confirmation will carry the time converted for you.

How do I join the webinar?

After you register, you'll receive an email with your personal join link. On Thursday morning, click it a few minutes before 7:00 AM Mountain. It opens in your browser, with no account or download required. A reminder goes out before we begin.

What if I can't attend live?

Register anyway, and do join us live if you possibly can. Being there in person is better: it's the only way to hear your question answered in the Q&A. But if, in the worst case, you have to miss it, that's fine. We'd still rather have you registered and hoping to come than not registered at all.

Is this only about cancer?

Cancer is the focus, because that's where the "what are my options?" question is most urgent. But Barbara teaches principles (causes, options, and how the body responds when supported) that apply to serious disease conditions more broadly, including diabetes, hypertension, and Lyme disease.

Will Barbara tell me to stop my treatment?

No. This webinar is about understanding your options and supporting your body well, alongside your own physician, never instead of them. Eden Valley's programs are designed to complement your medical care, and any change to treatment belongs in a conversation with your doctor.

Can I invite family or my church group?

Please do. Send them this page so each person registers with their own email and receives their own join link. Watching together and discussing afterward is a wonderful way to use the session.
